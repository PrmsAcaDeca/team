The Program Benefits Current Theme

Academic Excellence

The Academic Pentathlon is a five-event scholastic competition for middle school students. It challenges students to dive deep into five core subjects: Literature, Mathematics, Fine Arts, Science, and Social Science. At Paul Revere, we foster a culture of curiosity and rigorous study to prepare our students for local and national success.

Why Join the Team?

Boost Your GPA

Students develop advanced study habits and critical thinking skills that translate directly to higher performance in all school subjects.

Everyone Competes

The program is unique because teams must include students from all GPA levels (Honor, Scholastic, and Varsity), ensuring a diverse and inclusive environment.

Team Camaraderie

Beyond the books, you'll join a community of like-minded peers, attend study sessions, and build lasting friendships.

Future Benefits

Competing in the pentathlon can look good on applications to high school, college, or even universities across the whole country.

2025/2026 Theme: The Roaring Twenties

This year, our team explores the excitement and innovation of the 1920s. We are studying the science of electricity, the art of the Jazz Age, and historical turning points that shaped the modern world.
